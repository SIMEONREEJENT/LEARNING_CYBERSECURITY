# 🛡️ Introduction to Cyber Security

---

## 🔍 a. What is Cyber Security?

> Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting data on the internet and safeguarding digital assets—such as computers, mobile phones, laptops, and web applications—from potential threats and attacks.

Rather than being a single subject, it is a vast **"umbrella term"** that encompasses numerous sub-disciplines and methods dedicated to finding vulnerabilities and shielding systems from negative impacts.

---

## 🚨 b. Why is Cyber Security important?

Because we live in a digital era where personal data, such as hospital and health records, is stored online, cybersecurity has become a basic life skill necessary to protect yourself, your family, and your finances.

* **Financial Impact:** Cybercrimes are causing severe financial damage globally; for example, a news report indicated that victims in the state of Kerala alone lose an average of **70 lakh rupees daily** to cybercrimes.
* **Career Growth:** Aside from personal protection, cybersecurity is incredibly important because it provides a highly-paying, rapidly growing career path to combat the increasing volume of digital threats.

---

## ⚖️ c. Information Security vs Cyber Security

While the two terms are often used interchangeably, Cybersecurity is actually considered a specialized subset of Information Security. Here is a detailed breakdown of their differences and how they overlap:

* 🗄️ **Information Security (InfoSec):** Often referred to as "data security," this is a broad field focused on protecting **all data in any format**, whether it is physical (like paper documents in a filing cabinet) or digital. Its primary goal is to protect the **Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (the CIA Triad)** of information systems. The fundamental concepts of information security have deep historical roots, dating back to 1883 with the creation of algorithms and modern cryptography.
* 💻 **Cybersecurity:** This is a narrower, highly specialized practice focused explicitly on safeguarding **digital information stored on electronic systems** (such as networks, servers, mobile devices, and computers) against online malicious threats and unauthorized access. The true birth of cybersecurity began much later in the 1970s, alongside the development of ARPANET, the precursor to the internet.

### 🔄 Key Overlaps and Evolution

Despite their differences, both disciplines share fundamental goals. Both fields prioritize the value of the data to determine what needs to be protected most urgently. Furthermore, both require physical security components; for example, a robust cybersecurity strategy still relies on physical controls, like putting a padlock on a server room door to ensure unauthorized individuals cannot tamper with digital networks.

Because business operations have become heavily digitized over the last decade, the boundaries between Information Security and Cybersecurity are rapidly blurring, and the responsibilities of professionals in these roles are increasingly merging.

## 🏢 d. Cyber Security domains

Cybersecurity is divided into several specialized job roles and domains to tackle different aspects of security. The sources highlight the following key areas:

* ⚔️ **Penetration Testing:** Actively searching for vulnerabilities across web, Android, and iOS platforms to eliminate them before they are exploited.
* 🖥️ **Security Operations Center (SOC):** Continuously monitoring networks to detect and neutralize threats in real time.
* 📋 **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) & Auditing:** Verifying that proper security guidelines and organizational rules are correctly implemented to prevent threats.
* 🔎 **Forensics and Threat Intelligence:** Investigating cyber incidents and analyzing potential dangers.
